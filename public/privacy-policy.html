Overview

Poultry Hub is a bilingual (English / Urdu) app that publishes daily poultry market rates — chicken, meat, and eggs — by city. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled. It applies to the public app and to the admin panel used by our internal team to publish rates and news.

App reference: poultry.hub.updates.customer  |  Policy reference: PH-PP-2026-01

Data we collect

What we collect depends on whether you're using the public app or the admin panel.

Everyone who opens the app

On first launch, the app signs you in anonymously in the background (via Firebase Authentication) so we can tell installs apart — this does not involve a name, email, or phone number. We store a small record per install in our database (Cloud Firestore):

FieldWhat it is
Anonymous user IDA random identifier Firebase generates for your install; not linked to your identity.
First seen / last seenTimestamps of your first launch and most recent activity, used only for usage counts.
PlatformAndroid or iOS.
Preferred languageEnglish or Urdu, so your language choice can be restored on next launch.
Push notification tokenIf you allow notifications, a device token used to deliver rate-update alerts. No token means no push notifications.

Admin panel users only

Admin access requires phone number + one-time-passcode (OTP) sign-in via Firebase Phone Authentication, and only phone numbers on an internal allow-list can reach the admin panel. If you're an admin, we additionally store:

  • Your phone number, in E.164 format, used solely to authenticate you and identify who made a given rate/news edit.

Data we don't collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or payment information. The app has no in-app purchases.

How we use it

  • Operating the app: showing rates and news for your selected city, remembering your language and theme preference.
  • Sending you a push notification when a rate you follow is updated (only if you've granted notification permission).
  • Internal usage counts (how many installs are active), visible only to admins as a read-only list — no personal profile is built from this.
  • Securing the admin panel, so only allow-listed phone numbers can publish rates or news.

Third-party services

The app is built on Google's Firebase platform. Data described above is processed and stored using these Firebase/Google services, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

ServicePurpose
Firebase AuthenticationAnonymous sign-in for regular users; phone/OTP sign-in for admins.
Cloud FirestoreStores rates, news, cities, and the account records described above.
Firebase Cloud MessagingDelivers optional push notifications for rate updates.
Google AdMobServes the in-app ads that support the app — see below.

Advertising

The public app displays ads served by Google AdMob to support free access to the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID) and use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized advertising, in your device's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

We do not control what data AdMob collects for ad purposes — that's governed by Google's own privacy and ad policies, linked above.

Retention & security

Rate, news, and city data is retained indefinitely as historical market data (it powers the price-trend graphs). Anonymous install records are kept for as long as the app is installed. Access to the admin panel and its underlying database is restricted by Firestore security rules and a phone-number allow-list; only allow-listed admins can read or write rate and news data.

Your choices

  • Notifications: you can disable them any time in your device's app notification settings.
  • Language: switch between English and Urdu in the app's Settings tab at any time.
  • Deletion: to request deletion of your install's anonymous record or, if you're an admin, your account, contact us below.

Children's privacy

Poultry Hub is a general-audience market-information app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
